Temperature & Aeration Science

The Art of Ice & Glassware Pairing

Understand how thermal retention, glass rim thickness, and ice sphere surface area elevate every sip.

Sphere vs. Cube Dilution

Ice spheres feature a 24% lower surface-to-volume ratio than standard cubes, chilling high-proof spirits without watering down delicate botanical aromas.

Optimal Temp: -2°C Ideal for: Aged Spirits

Rim Thickness & Palate Flow

Laser-cut 1mm ultra-thin crystal rims deliver cocktail liquids directly to specific tongue taste receptors for heightened perception of sweetness and acidity.

Rim Width: 1.0mm Ideal for: Craft Sours

Aromatics & Bowl Curvature

Tapered coupe bowls concentrate volatile botanical esters near the nose, amplifying herbal notes in craft gins, vermouths, and dark spirits.

Angle: 35° Curve Ideal for: Botanicals
