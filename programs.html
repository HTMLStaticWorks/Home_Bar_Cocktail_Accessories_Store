Engineering Excellence

Materials & Finish Guide

Understand the food-grade metallurgy and vacuum PVD coating processes behind our long-lasting barware tools.

01

18/8 Food-Grade Stainless Steel

Corrosion-resistant steel alloy engineered to withstand acidic citrus juices, heavy shaking forces, and commercial dishwashing cycles.

02

Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD)

Titanium oxide vacuum coating creates scratch-proof gold, rose, and gunmetal finishes that won't chip, peel, or leach into liquids.

03

Precision Diamond Laser Etching

Sub-millimeter laser calibration lines inside our jiggers ensure exact liquid measurement tolerances for balanced flavor ratios.
